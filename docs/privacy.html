Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy covers Ridvi DV GK (Instagram @ridvidvgk, Facebook page "Ridvi DV GK") and the private tool Ridvi Uploader, operated by Vishal Rajput, Maharashtra, India (contact: vishalraajput24@gmail.com).

What Ridvi Uploader does

Ridvi Uploader is an internal tool used only by the owner to publish the owner's own educational cartoon videos (Indian history and general knowledge) to the owner's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook page. It is not offered to the public and no other user can sign in to it.

YouTube API Services

Ridvi Uploader uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos and set their titles and descriptions on the owner's channel. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google security settings.

Data we access and store

  • An OAuth refresh token for the owner's own Google account, stored only on the owner's private server, used solely to upload the owner's videos.
  • The upload result (video ID and status) in a private log.

We do not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or any third party. We do not read, store or analyse other users' YouTube data.

Instagram / Facebook

For automatic replies to comments we read the public comment text and username on our own posts only to reply to them; this data is not sold or shared. See also data deletion.

Deletion

To request deletion of any data related to you, email vishalraajput24@gmail.com. Revoking the Google permission immediately stops all YouTube access; stored tokens are then deleted within 7 days.
