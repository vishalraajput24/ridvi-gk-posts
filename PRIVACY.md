# Privacy Policy — Ridvi DV GK (@ridvidvgk)

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

**Ridvi DV GK** is a Marathi general-knowledge and current-affairs page on Instagram (@ridvidvgk) and Facebook ("Ridvi DV GK"). This app is used **only by the page owner** to publish our own posts/Reels/Stories and to reply to comments on our own posts.

## What data we use
- **Public comments and usernames** left on *our own* posts and Reels, and posts where our page is @mentioned — only to show a quiz score and reply (e.g. "3/3 correct").
- **Insights of our own page** (views, reach, likes) — only to see which posts work best.
- We do **not** collect passwords, phone numbers, email addresses, location or private messages for any other purpose.

## What we do NOT do
- We do **not** sell, rent or share any data with anyone.
- We do **not** use the data for advertising or profiling.
- We do **not** contact people who have not interacted with our page.

## Storage
Comment IDs and usernames are kept in a small private log on our own server only so the bot does not reply twice. Logs are deleted after 30 days.

## Delete your data
Send a direct message to **@ridvidvgk** on Instagram (or to the "Ridvi DV GK" Facebook page) saying **"DELETE MY DATA"** with your username. We will remove every record related to you within 7 days and confirm by reply. You can also simply delete your comment — we will not keep it.

## Contact
Instagram: [@ridvidvgk](https://www.instagram.com/ridvidvgk/) · Facebook page: Ridvi DV GK
